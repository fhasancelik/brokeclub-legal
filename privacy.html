🐷 Broke Club — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Broke Club ("the App") is developed by Hasan Çelik ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how. Short version: we collect as little as possible, the free version is supported by ads served through Google AdMob, Pro removes them, and we never sell your data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Display name. The name you type during onboarding. It is shown on challenge leaderboards and share cards. It can be any nickname — a real name is not required.
  • Challenge data. Challenges you create or join, daily check-ins ("held" / "slipped"), estimated daily amounts, and join codes.
  • Anonymous account identifier. The App signs you in anonymously (no email, phone number, or password is collected). A random identifier links your challenge data across sessions.
  • Push notification token. Only if you allow notifications. This is a random token issued by Expo's push service for your app install — it is not your phone number, device serial, or advertising ID. We use it to send occasional announcements and reminders, never ads. Turning notifications off in your device settings stops them; you can also ask us to delete the token using the contact address below.
  • Advertising data (free members only). To show ads, Google AdMob receives approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and which ads were shown, viewed or tapped; on Android also your device's advertising identifier (see section 3). Pro members are not shown ads and this data is not collected for them.
  • Penny AI chat (Pro, optional). If you use the in-app AI coach, your chat messages and a short summary from the App are processed as described in section 4.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password (sign-in is anonymous).
  • No bank or financial account access. Savings amounts are your own estimates; the App never connects to your bank.
  • No precise location, contacts, or photos.
  • No audio recordings. The microphone is only used, if you choose, to talk to Penny, and the audio is never saved (section 4).
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google may use the data described in section 1 to serve and measure ads, and to personalize them where you have allowed it. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. AdMob may also pass ad requests to its advertising partners.

  • iOS. Since version 1.7 the App does not ask for tracking permission and requests only non-personalized ads, so no advertising identifier is used and ads are not based on your activity in other apps.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads; ads are then no longer personalized.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland. We show a consent dialog before any ad request. You can revisit your choices anytime under You → Ad privacy options in the App.
  • Remove ads. Broke Club Pro removes every ad. Your purchase is handled by Apple or Google as described in section 6.

We also send anonymous ad-impression and subscription events to Meta (Facebook) to measure our own advertising campaigns. These events carry no name, email or advertising identifier from your device.

4. Penny AI Chat

Pro members can chat with Penny, the App's AI coach. The App asks for your permission before your first message. When you send a message, the App sends your recent chat messages together with a short summary from the App (your display name, currency, coaching tone, your current challenge's name, daily amount, streak and days left, your savings goal, the amount kept so far and your budget figures, if you use the Budget tab) to our server, which forwards them to the Claude API by Anthropic PBC to write Penny's reply.

  • We do not store your chat messages on our servers. We only record, per day, how many messages you sent and what processing them cost, linked to your anonymous account, to apply usage limits.
  • Anthropic processes the messages under its commercial terms and privacy policy; under those terms, API data is not used to train its models by default.
  • Your chat history is stored only on your device. New chat in the chat screen or You → Reset everything deletes it.
  • Talking by voice (optional). If you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech recognition turns what you say into text, which is then sent like a typed message. On iPhone this uses Apple's speech recognition, on the device where your language supports it and otherwise on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy; on Android it uses your device's speech recognition service (usually Google's). The App only uses the microphone while it is listening, never records or saves the audio, and never sends audio to us or to Anthropic. Penny's spoken replies are produced by your phone's built-in text-to-speech.
  • Please do not share card numbers, addresses, passwords or other sensitive personal details in the chat.
  • Penny is an AI and can be wrong. Its replies are not financial, legal, medical or other professional advice. If you are in danger or crisis, contact your local emergency number.

5. How Data Is Stored

Challenge and profile data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS, US East region) and protected with row-level security: only participants of a challenge can see that challenge's data. Data you keep on your device (streaks, preferences) stays on your device unless you publish a challenge or join one.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase receipts — never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

7. Sharing

When you share a flex card or an invite, the App creates content that you choose to share via your device's share sheet. We do not post anything on your behalf.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

You can wipe all local data anytime via You → Reset everything. To request deletion of cloud data (your anonymous profile, challenges, check-ins and Penny usage counts), contact us at the email below with your join code, and we will delete it within 30 days. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's own retention rules linked in section 3.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Ads are requested with Google's content rating capped for a general audience.
