🐷 Broke Club — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Broke Club ("the App") is developed by Hasan Çelik ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and how. Short version: we collect as little as possible, we don't run ads, and we never sell your data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Display name. The name you type during onboarding. It is shown on challenge leaderboards and share cards. It can be any nickname — a real name is not required.
  • Challenge data. Challenges you create or join, daily check-ins ("held" / "slipped"), estimated daily amounts, and join codes.
  • Anonymous account identifier. The App signs you in anonymously (no email, phone number, or password is collected). A random identifier links your challenge data across sessions.
  • Push notification token. Only if you allow notifications. This is a random token issued by Expo's push service for your app install — it is not your phone number, device serial, or advertising ID. We use it to send occasional announcements and reminders, never ads. Turning notifications off in iOS Settings stops them; you can also ask us to delete the token using the contact address below.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No email address, phone number, or password (sign-in is anonymous).
  • No bank or financial account access. Savings amounts are your own estimates; the App never connects to your bank.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. How Data Is Stored

Challenge and profile data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS, US East region) and protected with row-level security: only participants of a challenge can see that challenge's data. Data you keep on your device (streaks, preferences) stays on your device unless you publish a challenge or join one.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases; RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase receipts — never your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Sharing

When you share a flex card or an invite, the App creates content that you choose to share via the iOS share sheet. We do not post anything on your behalf.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You can wipe all local data anytime via Profile → Reset everything. To request deletion of cloud data (your anonymous profile, challenges, and check-ins), contact us at the email below with your join code, and we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
